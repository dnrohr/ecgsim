Attribution

ECGSIM was created as an interactive forward simulator for studying how myocardial transmembrane potentials generate ECG and body-surface potentials.

Data, manuals, cases, MATLAB helpers, tutorials, and papers were archived from ecgsim.org.

References

  • van Oosterom A, Oostendorp T. ECGSIM; an interactive tool for studying the genesis of QRST waveforms. Heart, 2004.
  • van Dam PM, van Oosterom A. Atrial Excitation Assuming Uniform Propagation. Journal of Cardiovascular Electrophysiology, 2003.
  • Patuwo, Wagner, Ajijola. Comparison of teaching basic ECG concepts with and without ECGSIM. Computers in Cardiology, 2007.

Known Limits

  • Use for education and research, not clinical diagnosis or inverse reconstruction.
  • Browser case opening is limited to supported bundles or generated bundle JSON files.
  • Lead polarity/reference semantics, arbitrary case fiducials, full legacy export parity, and movie export remain incomplete.
  • Recomputed adapted ECG traces are previews until WCT/reference lead transforms are fully decoded and parity-tested.

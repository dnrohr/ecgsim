Clipboard

The clipboard may be used to copy images from one application to another. In ECGSIM, you may copy the images in any of the four panes as an onto the clipboard by pressing Ctrl-C while the mouse is within the pane, or by selecting Copy from the menu that corresponds to the pane.

From the clipboard the image may be pasted into other applications. However, ECGSIM should still be running at the time that you paste the image. ECGSIM may be minimized during the paste operation.
